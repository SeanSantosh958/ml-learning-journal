## Key ideas
- A vector is an arrow or a list defined by it's coordinates from the origin.
- Vectors can be thought of in 3 ways, as arrows (Physics), as lists (CS), or abstract objects (Math)
- Vector addition = playing tip to tail
- Scalar multiplication = stretching or squishing a vector
